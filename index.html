About the Competition

Why We Draw for Nurses

"The trained nurse has become one of the great blessings of humanity."

Nurses are the pillars of our healthcare system — working tirelessly, sacrificing sleep, holding hands through pain, and being present in our most vulnerable moments. They are not just healthcare workers; they are guardians of life.

This drawing competition is a tribute — a canvas to express gratitude, admiration, and hope for the future of nursing. Through art, we amplify their voice and honour their immeasurable contribution to humanity.

How to Participate

Competition Guidelines

Follow these steps to submit your artwork and celebrate nursing through art.

1
Choose Your Theme

Select one of the four competition categories that resonates with you.

2
Create Your Artwork

Use any medium — pencil, watercolour, oil pastel, or digital art — on A4 or A3 paper. Must be original.

3
Label Your Drawing

Write your name, age, category, and school/institution clearly on the back of the drawing.

4
Submit & Celebrate

Register below and submit your scanned drawing before 30 April 2026. Winners will be announced on 12 May 2026!
