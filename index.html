About This Initiative

Why Nurses Are Our Future

This drawing competition — proudly presented by Priyanga — celebrates the unsung heroes who dedicate their lives to healing others. Through art, we honour their sacrifice, skill, and unwavering spirit.

Every drawing tells a story. Every brushstroke is a tribute. Together, we say: Empowered Nurses Save Lives.
