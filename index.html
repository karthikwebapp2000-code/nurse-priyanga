About the Competition

Why We Draw for Nurses

"The trained nurse has become one of the great blessings of humanity."

Nurses are the pillars of our healthcare system — working tirelessly, sacrificing sleep, holding hands through pain, and being present in our most vulnerable moments. They are not just healthcare workers; they are guardians of life.

This drawing competition is a tribute — a canvas to express gratitude, admiration, and hope for the future of nursing. Through art, we amplify their voice and honour their immeasurable contribution to humanity.

Why Nurses Matter

The Heart of Healthcare

🩺
First Responders

Nurses are often the first and last face patients see — providing immediate, life-saving care around the clock.

💊
Medication Heroes

Administering medications with precision and compassion, nurses prevent countless medical errors every single day.

🤝
Patient Advocates

Nurses speak up for patients who cannot speak for themselves — ensuring dignity, rights, and proper care at all times.

🌙
Night Watchers

While the world sleeps, nurses keep vigil — monitoring vitals, responding to alarms, and never leaving a patient alone.

🧠
Critical Thinkers

Split-second decisions, complex assessments, and quick thinking — nurses are the intelligent backbone of healthcare.

🌟
Future Leaders

Empowered nurses shape the future of medicine — innovating, educating, and leading the next generation of healers.

How to Participate

Competition Guidelines

Follow these simple steps to submit your artwork and be part of this wonderful celebration.

1
Choose Your Theme

Select one of the four competition categories that resonates with you — Superhero Nurse, Light in the Dark, Nurses of Tomorrow, or Heart of Healthcare.

2
Create Your Artwork

Use any medium — pencil, watercolour, oil pastel, digital art — on A4 or A3 paper. Your drawing must be original and created by you alone.

3
Label Your Drawing

Write your name, age, category, and school/institution clearly on the back of the drawing or in the submission form.

4
Submit & Celebrate

Register below and submit your scanned drawing. Winners will be announced and their artwork displayed on this website — celebrating nursing through your eyes!
