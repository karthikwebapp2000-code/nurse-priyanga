Our Mission

Why Empowered
Nurses Matter

Nurses are the heartbeat of healthcare — present in the most vulnerable and victorious moments of human life. When empowered with knowledge, resources, and recognition, they don't just save lives. They transform them.

"The trained nurse has become one of the great blessings of humanity, taking a place beside the physician and the priest."

— William Osler
🏥

Guardian of Life

Nurses constitute over 59% of global health professionals. They are the first and last line of compassionate care in every hospital, clinic and community.

✨

The Future is Nursing

With advanced education, technology and leadership, the next generation of nurses will redefine healthcare. This drawing competition celebrates that vision.

🎨

Art as Advocacy

Through drawing and visual art, we give a creative voice to the importance of nursing — inspiring young minds and honoring the heroes who heal.

Competition Details

How to Participate

Open to all artists, students and healthcare enthusiasts. Submit your drawing that celebrates the power, compassion and future of nursing.

01 🎨

Create Your Art

Draw your vision of an empowered nurse — on paper, digitally, or using mixed media. All artistic styles welcome. Let your creativity inspire others.

Any Medium
02 📸

Photograph & Submit

Take a clear photograph of your artwork and submit it via the online form below. Include your name, age, and a short description of your piece.

Online Submit
03 ⭐

Get Judged

Artwork will be judged on creativity, message clarity, emotional impact, and technical skill. Winners receive certificates, trophies and prizes.

Fair Judging
04 🏆

Win & Be Celebrated

Top entries featured in our exhibition, website gallery and social media. Three prize tiers: Gold, Silver and Bronze with special Priyanga Awards.

3 Prize Tiers
